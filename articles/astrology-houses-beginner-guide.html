Astrology Houses Beginner Guide

Learn the 12 houses and what life areas they represent.

Important disclaimer: This guide is for entertainment, journaling and self-reflection only. It is not professional advice.

Houses describe where a planetary theme appears in life. If planets are actors and signs are style, houses are the stage. A Venus placement in the 10th house may express love of beauty, harmony or values through career and public image.

The 12 houses

1stIdentity, body, first impression
2ndMoney, values, security
3rdCommunication, siblings, learning
4thHome, roots, family
5thCreativity, romance, joy
6thRoutines, work habits, health

8thShared resources, intimacy, transformation
9thBeliefs, travel, higher learning
10thCareer, reputation, public role
11thFriends, networks, future hopes
12thSolitude, dreams, unconscious patterns

Why birth time matters

House positions usually require accurate birth time and location. If birth time is unknown, house readings become unreliable.

How to use house meanings

Ask where the planet's theme becomes most visible. Mars in the 6th house may show drive through work habits; Mars in the 7th may show drive through relationships.

FAQ

Can I know houses without birth time?

Not reliably. Houses usually depend on time and place.

Is a planet in a house good or bad?

Neither. It shows a life area that carries more emphasis.

What is the most important house?

It depends on the chart and question.
