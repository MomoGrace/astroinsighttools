Lucky Colors by Zodiac Sign

A practical guide to zodiac colors and how to use them as intention cues.

Important disclaimer: This guide is for entertainment, journaling and self-reflection only. It is not medical, legal, financial, psychological or life-decision advice.
In this guideColor ideas by elementSimple practice

Lucky colors by zodiac sign are best used as design language. They can help you choose a mood for a workspace, journal page or outfit. They should not be used as a promise of success.

Simple practice

Pick one color for the week and connect it to one habit: focus, rest, money organization, communication or creative work.
