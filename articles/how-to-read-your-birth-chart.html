How to Read Your Birth Chart: A Complete Beginner's Guide to Understanding Your Natal Chart

Learn to interpret the three levels of your birth chart, including houses, planets, and aspects, and understand what your cosmic blueprint may reveal about you.

What Your Birth Chart Is

A natal chart is a symbolic map of the sky at your birth moment and location. It is best used as a reflective tool, not fixed destiny.

What You Need

  • Birth date
  • Birth time
  • Birth location

Reading the Chart in Layers

Layer 1: Zodiac Signs

Signs describe style and flavor.

Layer 2: Planets

Planets describe life functions: Sun (identity), Moon (emotion), Mercury (mind), Venus (values), Mars (action), and outer planets for broader themes.

Layer 3: Houses

Houses describe life areas where those energies show up.

Layer 4: Aspects

Aspects (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition) describe interaction between parts of the chart.

Step-by-Step Method

  1. Find your Big Three.
  2. Read Mercury, Venus, and Mars.
  3. Check house placements.
  4. Study major aspects.
  5. Identify repeating patterns.

Important Reminders

Astrology is symbolic and non-deterministic. Contradictions are normal. Context and lived experience matter.
