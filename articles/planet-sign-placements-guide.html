Planet Sign Placements Guide

Learn what it means when a planet is in a zodiac sign.

Important disclaimer: This guide is for entertainment, journaling and self-reflection only. It is not professional advice.
In this guidePlanet and sign formulaPlanet meaningsBest way to read placements

A planet sign placement combines two symbols: the planet describes the life function, and the sign describes the style. Venus shows love and pleasure; Scorpio gives intensity and depth. Together, Venus in Scorpio describes a love style that may be private, loyal and emotionally all-or-nothing.

Planet and sign formula

Use this simple formula: Planet equals what, sign equals how. Mercury is thinking and communication. Mercury in Gemini communicates quickly and curiously. Mercury in Capricorn communicates carefully and practically.

Best way to read placements

Start with the planet's function. Then add the sign's style. Finally, ask how this pattern appears in everyday choices.

FAQ

Do I need an API to interpret placements?

No. If the user already knows the placement, an interpretation tool can explain it without calculating a chart.

What if I do not know my planet signs?

You need a chart calculator or astrology app to find them accurately.

Can one placement explain everything?

No. A full chart includes many placements and relationships.
