Chinese Zodiac for Global Readers

A simple global-friendly introduction to the Chinese zodiac and its 12 animals.

Important disclaimer: This guide is for entertainment, journaling and self-reflection only. It is not medical, legal, financial, psychological or life-decision advice.
In this guideThe 12 animalsYear boundaries matter

The Chinese zodiac is a 12-year symbolic cycle, with each year linked to an animal. Global readers can enjoy it without treating it as fixed destiny.

The 12 animals

Year boundaries matter

The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, so the new year usually begins in late January or February.
