Zodiac Money Style Guide

Use zodiac elements to reflect on spending, saving and financial habits without replacing advice.

Money habits are shaped by upbringing, income, stress and values. Zodiac signs cannot replace financial advice, but they can help you reflect on spending, saving and emotional patterns.

Element money patterns

Fire may spend on experience and momentum. Earth may value security and tangible assets. Air may spend on learning, technology or social life. Water may spend from emotion, comfort or care.

Important note
