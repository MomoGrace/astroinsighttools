Zodiac Compatibility Explained: Why Sun Signs Aren't Everything and What Really Matters

Go beyond sun sign compatibility. Learn how moon signs, Venus signs, Mars signs, aspects, and synastry can reveal a more complete relationship picture.

Why Sun Signs Are Not Enough

Sun signs matter, but they do not fully explain communication style, emotional needs, values, or conflict patterns.

Key Compatibility Factors

Moon

Emotional comfort and private needs.

Venus

Love language, values, and relationship preferences.

Mars

Passion, drive, and conflict style.

Mercury

Communication and decision-making style.

Rising Sign

First impressions and day-to-day approach.

Synastry and Aspects

Comparing two charts (synastry) shows how planetary energies interact through conjunctions, trines, sextiles, squares, and oppositions.

Important Truths

  • No perfect match exists.
  • Compatibility is not destiny.
  • Respect, communication, and effort matter more than any single placement.
