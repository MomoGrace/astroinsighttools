Editorial Policy

How we create and maintain clear, safe and useful content.

Astro Insight Tools publishes symbolic astrology and numerology content for entertainment and self-reflection. We do not publish medical, legal, financial, or mental health advice.

How content is created

Tool descriptions and guides are drafted to be practical, beginner-friendly and non-fatalistic. We focus on reflection prompts, plain language and realistic boundaries.

Quality standards

  • Explain what a tool can and cannot do.
  • Avoid fear-based claims, guarantees and manipulative language.
  • Use consistent definitions across tools and guides.
  • Update pages when wording is unclear or outdated.

Update cycle

Core pages are reviewed regularly for clarity, broken links and consistency. High-traffic pages are reviewed first when feedback identifies confusion.

Corrections and feedback

If you find an error or unclear statement, email AstroInsightTools@proton.me with the page URL and suggested correction.
