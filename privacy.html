Privacy Policy

How Astro Insight Tools handles browser-based inputs, cookies, advertising and privacy.

Astro Insight Tools is a static website that provides free astrology, zodiac, numerology, compatibility and personality tools for entertainment, journaling and self-reflection.

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser where possible. We do not ask users to create an account, and we do not intentionally store personal calculator results on our server.

This website may use third-party services such as analytics tools, advertising services or performance services to understand website usage and support the operation of the site.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users may also choose to disable cookies through their browser settings.

Where required by law, we show consent options before loading non-essential cookies and personalized advertising technologies.

Third-party vendors and ad networks may also use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to collect information as part of ad serving, analytics or website performance measurement.

We do not provide medical, legal, financial, psychological or professional advice. The tools and content on this website are for entertainment and self-reflection only.

For privacy questions, contact Astro Insight Tools at AstroInsightTools@proton.me.

Cookie and Tracking Technologies

How We Use Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Understand how visitors use our site
  • Remember user preferences
  • Support advertising services, including Google AdSense if enabled
  • Measure website performance
  • Improve user experience and website security

Google Analytics and Website Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or similar website analytics tools to understand:

  • How many people visit our site
  • Which pages are most popular
  • How long visitors spend on pages
  • What countries or regions our visitors come from
  • Which devices and browsers are commonly used

Analytics tools may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information. Users can:

  • Disable cookies in their browser settings
  • Use browser extensions to block tracking
  • Review Google’s privacy and advertising settings

Third-Party Advertising Services

If we enable Google AdSense or other advertising networks, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Specifically:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings
  • Users may also choose to disable cookies through their browser settings
  • Some advertising cookies may still be used for purposes such as frequency capping, ad reporting, fraud prevention, security, and ad performance measurement

Personalized Advertising Opt-Out

Users may control or opt out of personalized advertising through:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Browser cookie settings
  • Available privacy controls on their device or browser

Please note that disabling personalized ads does not necessarily remove all ads. It may result in less relevant ads.

GDPR and International Privacy Laws

For users in the European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other regions where cookie consent is required, we aim to follow applicable consent requirements before using non-essential cookies, personalized advertising technologies, or analytics cookies where required by law.

Where a cookie consent banner or consent option is shown, users may choose whether to accept or reject non-essential cookies according to the available options.

If you do not want cookies to be stored, you may also disable cookies through your browser settings.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal data collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable cookies in your browser
  • Withdraw consent where consent is required
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions

To exercise these rights or ask privacy questions, contact us at AstroInsightTools@proton.me
